Hanuman Chalisa

baranauँ raghubara bimala jasu jo daayaku phala chaari

buddhiheena tanu jaanike sumiraum pavanakumaara

bala budhi bidyaa dehu mohim harahu kalesa bikaara

jaya hanumaana jnyaana guna saagara

jaya kapeesa tihun loka ujaagara

raama doota atulita bala dhaamaa

mahaabeera bikrama bajaramgee

kumati nivaara sumati ke samgee

kamchana barana biraaja subesaa

kaanana kumdala kumchita kesaa

haatha bajra au dhvajaa biraajai

kaandhe moonja janeoo saajai

samkara suvana kesaree namdana

teja prataapa mahaa jaga bamdana

bidyaavaana gunee ati chaatura

raama kaaja karibe ko aatura

prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiyaa

raama lakhana seetaa mana basiyaa

sookshma roopa dhari siyahim dikhaavaa

bikata roopa dhari lamka jaraavaa

bheema roopa dhari asura samhaare

raamachamdra ke kaaja samvaare

laaya sajeevana lakhana jiyaaye

shreeraghubeera harashi ura laaye

raghupati keenhee bahuta bada़ाee

tuma mama priya bharatahi sama bhaaee

sahasa badana tumharo jasa gaavaim

asa kahi shreepati kamtha lagaavaim

sanakaadika brahmaadi muneesaa

naarada saarada sahita aheesaa

jama kubera digapaala jahaan te

kabi kobida kahi sake kahaan te

tuma upakaara sugreevahim keenhaa

raama milaaya raaja pada deenhaa

tumharo mamtra bibheeshana maanaa

lamkesvara bhae saba jaga jaanaa

juga sahasra jojana para bhaanoo

leelyo taahi madhura phala jaanoo

prabhu mudrikaa meli mukha maaheem

jaladhi laamghi gaye acharaja naaheem

durgama kaaja jagata ke jete

sugama anugraha tumhare tete

raama duaare tuma rakhavaare

hota na aajnyaa binu paisaare

saba sukha lahai tumhaaree saranaa

tuma rakshaka kaahoo ko dara naa

aapana teja samhaaro aapai

teenom loka haanka te kaanpai

bhoota pishaacha nikata nahim aavai

mahaabeera jaba naama sunaavai

naasai roga harai saba peeraa

japata niramtara hanumata beeraa

samkata te hanumaana chhuda़ाvai

mana krama bachana dhyaana jo laavai

saba para raama tapasvee raajaa

tina ke kaaja sakala tuma saajaa

aura manoratha jo koee laavai

soi amita jeevana phala paavai

chaarom juga parataapa tumhaaraa

hai parasiddha jagata ujiyaaraa

saadhu samta ke tuma rakhavaare

asura nikamdana raama dulaare

ashta siddhi nava nidhi ke daataa

asa bara deena jaanakee maataa

raama rasaayana tumhare paasaa

sadaa raho raghupati ke daasaa

tumhare bhajana raama ko paavai

janama janama ke dukha bisaraavai

jahaan janma haribhakta kahaaee

aura devataa chitta na dharaee

hanumata sei sarba sukha karaee

samkata katai mitai saba peeraa

jo sumirai hanumata balabeeraa

krupaa karahu guru deva kee naaeeं

jo sata baara paatha kara koee

chhootahi bamdi mahaa sukha hoee

jo yaha padha़ै hanumaana chaaleesaa

hoya siddhi saakhee gaureesaa

tulaseedaasa sadaa hari cheraa

pavanatanaya samkata harana mamgala moorati roopa

raama lakhana seetaa sahita hrudaya basahu sura bhoopa

With the dust of the Guru's lotus feet I clear the mirror of my mind, and I sing the pure glory of Rama, which gives the four fruits of life.
Knowing this body to be without wisdom, I remember the son of the wind. Give me strength, wisdom and learning, and take away my sorrow and weakness.

Victory to Hanuman, an ocean of wisdom and virtue. Victory to the lord of the vanaras, the light of the three worlds.
You are Rama's messenger, a home of strength beyond compare. You are Anjana's son, and your name is the son of the wind.
Great hero, mighty Bajrang. You drive away a crooked mind and stay with a clear one.
Your body shines like gold, and you are beautifully dressed. Earrings hang at your ears, and your hair is curled.
A vajra and a banner shine in your hands. A sacred thread of munja grass rests on your shoulder.
You are Shankara's son and Kesari's joy. The world bows to your light and your valour.
You are learned, gifted and very skilful, and eager to do Rama's work.
You love to hear the Lord's story. Rama, Lakshmana and Sita live in your heart.
In a tiny form you appeared before Sita. In a fierce form you burned Lanka.
In a terrible form you destroyed the demons, and you completed Rama's work.
You brought the sanjivani and restored Lakshmana. Joyful, Sri Raghuvira held you to his heart.
Raghunatha praised you greatly: you are as dear to me as my brother Bharata.
A thousand mouths sing your glory. Saying this, the Lord of Sri drew you to his throat.
Sanaka and the other sages, Brahma and the great seers, Narada, Saraswati and the king of serpents,
Yama, Kubera and the guardians of the directions — even poets and scholars cannot finish telling your glory.
You did a great kindness for Sugriva. You joined him with Rama and he received the kingdom.
Vibhishana accepted your counsel and became lord of Lanka. The whole world came to know it.
The sun is thousands of yojanas away. You swallowed it, taking it for a sweet fruit.
With the Lord's ring held in your mouth, you leapt the ocean. There was nothing to wonder at.
However hard a task in the world may be, it becomes easy by your grace.
You keep the gate of Rama's door. No one enters without your leave.
All happiness is found in your refuge. You are the protector; there is no one to fear.
You alone hold your own radiance. The three worlds tremble at your call.
Ghosts and restless spirits do not come near when the name of the great hero is heard.
Disease leaves and every pain is taken away when the hero Hanuman is remembered without ceasing.
Hanuman frees a person from danger when mind, deed and word are set on him.
Rama is the king above all, the man of tapas. You fulfil every work of his.
Whatever wish a person brings, that person receives the boundless fruit of life.
Your glory stands in all four ages. It is famous, and it lights the world.
You guard the good and the saints. You are the end of demons, and Rama's beloved.
Mother Janaki gave you the boon of being the giver of the eight powers and the nine treasures.
You hold the elixir of Rama's name. Remain forever the servant of Raghunatha.
By singing of you, a person reaches Rama, and forgets the sorrow of birth after birth.
At the last hour one goes to Raghuvira's city, and wherever one is born one is called a devotee of Hari.
The heart does not cling to any other god. Serving Hanuman brings every happiness.
Danger is cut away and every pain ends for the one who remembers the mighty Hanuman.
Victory, victory, victory to Hanuman, lord of the vanaras. Be gracious, as a guru and a god.
Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bondage and comes to great happiness.
Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa gains the fulfilment of which Gauri's lord is witness.
Tulsidas is forever Hari's servant. Make your home, O lord, in this heart.
Son of the wind, remover of danger, form of all that is auspicious: live in the heart together with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita, king of the gods.
