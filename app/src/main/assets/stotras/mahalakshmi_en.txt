Salutation to the great Maya, worshipped by the gods on the seat of Sri. Conch, discus and mace in your hands: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Salutation to you who ride Garuda and who end Kolasura's threat. Goddess who takes away every wrong: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
All-knowing, giver of every wish, the end of what is harmful. Goddess who takes away every sorrow: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Goddess who gives success and wisdom, enjoyment and liberation. You are the mantra itself: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Goddess without beginning or end, the first power, great ruler. Born of yoga and filled with yoga: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Gross and subtle, greatly fierce, great power and great support. Goddess who takes away a great wrong: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Goddess seated on a lotus, whose form is the supreme Brahman. Supreme ruler, mother of the world: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Goddess dressed in white and adorned with many ornaments. You abide as the world, mother of the world: Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.
Whoever recites these eight verses with devotion gains every fulfilment, and a kingdom, always.
Read once a day, and a great wrong falls away. Read twice a day, and wealth and grain gather.
Read three times a day, and a great enemy is ended. Mahalakshmi remains gracious, the giver of what is asked, and auspicious.
